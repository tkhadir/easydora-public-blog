# What's New — March 7, 2026
Hey there! We made EasyDORA better! Here is what changed 
---
## The charts actually move now!
You know those pretty line charts that show how your team is doing over time?Before, they looked like a **flat boring line** — because they only had one or two dots. Not fun at all. 
Now the charts show **lots of dots** spread across the whole time period you picked — so you can really see if things are getting better or worse. It's like watching a rollercoaster instead of a flat road! 
This works on both the page for **one project** and the page that shows **all your projects together**.
---
## The Quick Check page stopped being grumpy
The Quick Check page used to show a scary red error message for no good reason — even when you were looking at a project that everyone can see. It would say things like *"401"* or *"403"* which just means it couldn't find its key to open the door. 
We fixed it! Now it tries **three different keys** one after another until one works. Much friendlier. 
---
## Cleaned up the "All Projects" page
The big overview page that shows all your projects at once had four separate charts that looked a little different from the rest of the app. We replaced them with **one nice chart** that matches everything else. Tidier and easier to read! 
---
## Squashed some bugs
- Fixed a bug that made the app crash during building - Fixed a missing file that was causing a small warning 
---
## Everything is tested and working
We ran all the checks before shipping this. **27 tests passed, 0 failed.** All good!
